Why I Enjoy Music

Interests | My Hero: Taylor Swift

Music is one of my favorite interests because it can change my mood and help me express emotions. I enjoy listening to different styles, including pop, rock, and acoustic music. Each type of music has its own sound and can be interesting for a different situation.

I especially like music because it helps me relax while I am studying, traveling, or spending time with friends. I also enjoy paying attention to the lyrics, instruments, and melodies in a song. Music can connect people even when they have different backgrounds and experiences.
